LinkedIn to Sheets Privacy Policy

This extension collects publicly available LinkedIn profile information (such as name, job title, company, location, and profile URL) only when the user actively uses the extension.

The data is used exclusively to populate a Google Sheet selected by the user.

No data is stored externally, sold, or shared with third parties.

All processing occurs locally within the extension and the user’s Google account.

The extension does not collect sensitive personal data, financial data, or authentication credentials.
