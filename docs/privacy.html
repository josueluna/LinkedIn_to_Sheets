Privacy Policy

How LinkedIn to Sheets handles your data — short version: it doesn't leave your browser.

What we collect

Only publicly available LinkedIn profile information — name, job title, company, location, and profile URL — and only when you actively trigger the extension.

How it's used

Captured data is used exclusively to populate the Google Sheet you select. Nothing else — no analytics, no profiling, no secondary use.

No external storage

No data is stored externally, sold, or shared with third parties. All processing occurs locally within the extension and your own Google account.

What we never touch

The extension does not collect sensitive personal data, financial data, or authentication credentials of any kind.

Questions about your data?

We're happy to answer any privacy questions directly. Reach out via the support form.
