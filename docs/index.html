The problem

You know the drill: open profile → copy → switch tab → paste → repeat. Hundreds of times a week. It's slow, repetitive, and honestly — a terrible use of a recruiter's time.

  • · Switching tabs over and over
  • · Typos and inconsistent formatting
  • · Duplicate candidates slipping through

The solution

LinkedIn to Sheets removes the most repetitive part of sourcing. You stay on LinkedIn — the data goes where it needs to go. No friction, no switching tabs, no manual errors.

  • ✓ One click captures the full profile
  • ✓ Customisable column mapping
  • ✓ Warns you if the candidate already exists

How it works

From profile to spreadsheet in four steps

No setup headaches, no API keys. Just install, connect, and start sourcing faster.

Step 01

Install the extension

Add LinkedIn to Sheets from the Chrome Web Store in seconds.

Step 02

Connect Google Sheets

Sign in with Google and pick the spreadsheet and tab you want to fill.

Step 03

Open any LinkedIn profile

Browse LinkedIn the way you always do. The extension reads the profile in view.

Step 04

Click 'Paste Profile'

Name, company, title, location and profile URL land in the right columns instantly.
